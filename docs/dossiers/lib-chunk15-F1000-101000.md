## Adversarial corrections (code-region R1)
The R1 verifier disproved 3 boundaries against the raw bytes; all fixed and re-gated:
1. **`0xF286C` preamble-orphan** — `lui/lw $v0` at `0xF286C/F2870` (read at `0xF28AC`
   before any write) was wrongly attached to the tail of `func_000F26DC` (which already
   returned at `0xF2864`). Moved the boundary back to `0xF286C`; new
   `func_000F286C` `[0xF286C,0xF2C98)`.
2. **`0xF4AFC` preamble-orphan** — `mtc1 $a1,$f4` at `0xF4AFC` (read at
   `c.lt.s $f4,$f2@0xF4B24` before any write) was wrongly attached to `func_000F4AA4`'s
   tail. Moved back; new `func_000F4AFC` `[0xF4AFC,0xF4F04)`.
3. **`0xF8480` missed frameless leaf** — `func_000F8310` returns at `0xF8478`+delay; the
   big-endian word-loader at `0xF8480..0xF84AC` is a separate callable leaf (not
   fall-through-reachable, not a branch target). Split out as `func_000F8480`.
