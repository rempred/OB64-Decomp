# W8 paired reduction R1 independent review

## Verdict

**Accepted** at commit `b2f7ac0a06cf6469b471cd04843a5b44069181c1`.

The commit adds a bounded, input-specific allocation study, eight cumulative common deletions,
two preserved reduced research sources, and their neutral observation records. The authoritative
run retains the directional integer-endpoint allocation effect across every deletion: the shared
form has an accessed endpoint HOME and the localized form has real endpoint consumers without a
HOME. No required fix remains.

## Review findings and corrections

The first review pass found that each top-level trial row redundantly placed the complete pair under
both `left` and `right`, obscuring the per-side result identity. The final driver emits flat per-side
rows, asserts that shape, and preserves the earlier run only as superseded ignored evidence.

A follow-up adversarial check found that the allocation predicate counted a destination-only lreg
definition as a real consumer. With a HOME and unrelated stack loads/stores, that malformed fixture
could satisfy the prior predicate. The final implementation inspects balanced `ior:SI` source
subtrees in initial and lreg dumps. Its destination-only negative rejects, while fresh `replay-r2`
retains the same eight study outcomes.

## Independent checks

- `node build/w8-paired-reduction-review-r1/checks.cjs` passed at the accepted commit. It bound all
  21 recorded inputs and 42 compiler invocations to their per-input analysis files, source and
  expanded bytes, four current dependency identities, authenticated pinned/tracer binaries,
  successful commands, and exact pinned/tracer emission agreement.
- The independent predicate checks confirmed a newly located endpoint for every side and trial,
  real OR-source consumers after allocation, a unique shared HOME slot with both emitted loads and
  stores, and no local HOME. Same-pair, reversed, short-endpoint, and destination-only controls all
  reject. The selected endpoints are input-specific pseudos 650/654; the shared slot is 428 and the
  selected frames are 480/472.
- Each trial removes one nonempty contiguous byte range, and the removed bytes are identical on both
  sides. The eight cumulative deletions remove exactly 3,201 bytes and 64 newline-terminated lines
  per side. Every intermediate preserves its side's exact packet/row-loop core hash. The selected
  archives are byte-identical to the final reduced inputs.
- A manual source-flow review found no read made undefined by the deletions. Removed declarations are
  confined to their removed blocks or become unused; retained color, factor, header, stride,
  capacity, geometry, endpoint, packet, and row-loop values still receive their original
  definitions. The deletions intentionally remove rendering side effects, so this check supports a
  compiler research artifact rather than game-behavior equivalence.
- Fresh source-policy classification of both tracked reductions reproduced `PURE_C`, exact source
  and expanded identities, all four dependencies, and full preprocessor provenance from the curated
  observations. Their parent and pair relations are correct, and both metadata records bind the
  final study-driver hash.
- The restored full sources reproduce the before-run source and pinned assembly hashes. Those
  assembly hashes also equal the retained R7 `e1p1t0` and `i1t0` pinned outputs. All twenty protected
  W8 inputs reauthenticated unchanged. `node tests/w8_paired_reduction.js` reports 12 focused
  controls passing, and `git diff --check` passes for the accepted commit.

## Acceptance limits

The study establishes that the eight removed common units are unnecessary for the observed R7
shared/local endpoint-HOME distinction in these exact full-function compiler inputs. It does not
identify which remaining lifetime or consumer relation causes the difference, prove minimality, or
generalize to the D037 context. Pseudo numbers, stack offsets, and frame sizes remain properties of
the authenticated inputs and toolchain.

The reduced sources deliberately omit matrix, color, scale, preload, and graphics-command effects.
They are preserved research artifacts, not production replacements. This acceptance does not
establish original-source identity, semantic equivalence, matching C, ownership, relocation
correctness, target-byte equality, complete-ROM equality, or W8 completion. No runtime, full-ROM
verifier, or structural audit was required because the commit changes no production or structural
foundation.
