- B06C uses ten entries, 40 bytes and alignment eight. Its preserved contract needs neither `sourceObjectPrefix` nor repeated compiler occurrences.
- EF50 uses seven entries plus four native trailing alignment bytes, totaling 32. Existing `trailingPaddingBytes` expresses this producer shape directly.
- `sourceObjectPrefix` is not a generic slice escape. It requires compiler-occurrence grammar, a zero-offset complete prefix, necessary terminal zero alignment, and a retained exterior tail of exactly that padding size. EF50's 1,112-byte final tail cannot satisfy a four-byte prefix-tail substitution. No such substitution is needed.
- `compilerOccurrences` describes actual repeated compiler sections from one producer. It cannot join B06C's and EF50's separate producers. Without `sourceObjectPrefix`, at least two occurrences are required, with complete entry relocations and authenticated alignment grammar.
- The middle D14C interval contains five entries, four zero bytes, then ten entries: `20 + 4 + 40 = 64`. The placement-only D14C fixture expresses offsets zero and 24 with `paddingBefore` at offset 20. It does not establish exact D14C compilation. A later D14C C contribution must replace, not overlap, the retained interior.
- `preservedInteriorBefore` requires the entire original row to have no nonempty actual REL/RELA sections targeting it. The accepted review tested rejection even for a relocation outside the retained interval. Generated retained objects must be read-only, alignment-one PROGBITS with no real relocations or named non-section symbols.

Concrete rejected alternatives are keeping B06C's old tail, treating nonzero D14C bytes as compiler padding, assigning an exterior prefix to EF50, or overlapping future D14C C with retained ASM. Relocation-bearing original expressions remain unsupported. No evidence here calls for a symbol-specific bypass or speculative tooling change.
