Story sound is the smallest standalone target.** The first choices below and the additional army record owners are available to contributors. Use the exact workbench symbols in the [target list](../../config/matching-priorities.json). Announce the owners you plan to cover through the [contributor guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#find-or-request-a-target).

**C interface follow-up for sound command `0x6F`.** The accepted dispatcher passes two 32-bit arguments, context and gain. The accepted wrapper `002831E0` declares one, and so do its two gain-service declarations. Retail instructions preserve the incoming gain in `$a1` through either call to `0x800EA838` or `0x800EA884`. Replaying the original instructions confirmed the exact gain in all 48 tested context/value combinations. Preserve both arguments in Editor host adaptations. Reconciling the Decomp C declarations is a separate interface task on already matching code. ## Army hook coverage

The two Editor catalogs identify 102 hook sites across 61 distinct owners. Each site’s original instruction words and its catalog owner’s digest were checked against retail. Both catalogs are needed to count the four sites in `0021C140` and the four in `002335E0`.
